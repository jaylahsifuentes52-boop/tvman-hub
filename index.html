❓ Frequently Asked Questions

                        

                            Got questions about mods, menus, or releases? Find quick answers to common questions below!
                        

                        

                            

                                
Q: How do I download/get the modded APKs?

                                
A: All free modded APKs and project files are distributed directly through our Discord community server. Join the Discord to access the download channels.

                            

                            

                                
Q: Is TvMenu Lib free to use?

                                
A: Yes! TvMenu Lib is completely free and intended for modding Gorilla Tag copies and fan games. Check the library tab for instructions.

                            

                            

                                
Q: When is TvMenuPCVR releasing?

                                
A: TvMenuPCVR is currently untested and coming soon. Keep an eye on the Discord announcements channel for release dates and testing info!

                            

                            

                                
Q: Who do I contact if I need support?

                                
A: You can reach out via email at TvmanMods@proton.me or open a support ticket inside our Discord server.
